What is NoraOS?

NoraOS is a personal project to build a small, cozy desktop world you can explore and get a little lost in. It takes place inside a fictional operating system, inspired by Windows XP but with its own style and personality.

The OS is filled with simple apps, photos, emails, and documents that hint at the people who used this digital space— and the strange events that led to its current state.

Currently building early prototypes and UI experiments.

Planned features

NoraOS is still taking shape, but these are the ideas guiding the design right now.

Desktop & apps

Simple windowed apps, a file explorer, notes, and a few playful utilities to discover over time.

Gentle narrative

Optional story threads hidden in emails, logs, and photos—easy to follow, easy to ignore.

Low‑friction sessions

Short play sessions that save what you moved, opened, or pinned for next time.
