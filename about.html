About NoraOS

NoraOS is a game about an abandoned operating system that has a ton of secrets. The main antagonist is the Nora Virtual Assistant, who has its own chat app within the game. When you say that you have to go, Nora loses her mind — though being an AI herself and having no mind, she does indeed have one!

The game was made in Godot, and it was created by music artist and YouTuber MJOStudios, who is also the creator of the analog horror series THE CULT OF KAIDR. MJOStudios, also known as Joseph Sanders, is a neurodiverse high school student with a deep passion for technology and coding.
